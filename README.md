### 📌 Featured Projects

#### 🔹 Mood-Based Recommendation Platform — Final-Year Capstone

*Classifies a user's emotional state from live webcam input, then recommends music, film, and humor matched to the detected mood.*

- **What it does:** facial expression recognition drives recommendations across three content types from a single inferred mood, with an anonymous community feed alongside it
- **Tech:** Python, PyTorch, OpenCV, MLflow
- [**View Project**](https://github.com/dvadashpurani/REPLACE-WITH-REAL-REPO)

#### 🔹 Voice-Enabled Chess Engine

*Play a full game of chess by speaking your moves.*

- **What it does:** minimax search with alpha–beta pruning over a custom board evaluation function, plus a speech-recognition layer for spoken move entry
- **Tech:** Python, python-chess, SpeechRecognition
- [**View Project**](https://github.com/dvadashpurani/portfolio-dvadash/tree/main/projects/aiml/Voice%20-Ai%20-chess)

#### 🔹 Customer Segmentation (RFM Analysis)

*Segmenting ~5,000 customers from five years of messy transactional data.*

- **What it does:** cleaning and normalizing real transactional records, then RFM clustering into behavioral cohorts used for targeted outreach
- **Tech:** Python, pandas, scikit-learn, MongoDB, Power BI
- [**View Project**](https://github.com/dvadashpurani/REPLACE-WITH-REAL-REPO)

#### 🔹 Image Classification with Deep CNNs

*Multi-class image classification on 10,000+ labeled images.*

- **What it does:** convolutional networks with normalization and data augmentation, evaluated against a held-out test split
- **Tech:** Python, TensorFlow, Keras
- [**View Project**](https://github.com/dvadashpurani/REPLACE-WITH-REAL-REPO)
