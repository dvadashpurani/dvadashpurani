### 📌 Featured Projects

#### 🔹 Unsaid: Target-Aware Moderation

*A trust-and-safety tool for peer-support communities that can tell "I feel worthless" from "you are worthless".*

- **What it does:** works out who hostile language is aimed at, combining hand-written rules with a model trained on 116,000 human-rated comments, and sends posts it isn't sure about to a human moderator. On a held-out set it cut wrongful blocks of people describing their own distress from 59% to 3% compared with a word filter.
- **Tech:** Python, scikit-learn, NLP, Flask, MongoDB, Docker
- [**Live demo**](https://unsaid-xdsa.onrender.com) · [**Demo video and write-up**](https://dvadash-portfolio.vercel.app/#projects) · code available on request

#### 🔹 Voice-Enabled Chess Engine

*Play a full game of chess against a search-based AI, with the mouse or by speaking your moves.*

- **What it does:** negamax search with alpha–beta pruning, a transposition table and an opening book over a hand-written evaluation function, plus a speech-recognition layer that turns spoken squares into legal moves
- **Tech:** Python, Pygame, SpeechRecognition
- [**View Project**](https://github.com/dvadashpurani/Voice-ai-chess) · [**Demo video**](https://dvadash-portfolio.vercel.app/#projects)

#### 🔹 Customer Segmentation (RFM Analysis) — internship project

*Built at Konkem Industries so the sales team could see which customers to prioritise.*

- **What it does:** scores 5,000+ customers on purchase value and payment behaviour, groups them into 4 value-based segments, and gives the sales team a dashboard to search and compare customers. The public version runs on generated dummy data.
- **Tech:** Python, pandas, Flask, MongoDB, React
- [**View Project**](https://github.com/dvadashpurani/rfm-analysis) · [**Demo video**](https://dvadash-portfolio.vercel.app/#projects)
